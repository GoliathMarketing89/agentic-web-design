Why these skills exist

AI can build a website in minutes. The problem was never speed. It was taste.

Every coding agent ships the same default: a blue-purple gradient hero, a generic sans-serif, a layout that looks like every other AI site on the internet. It's technically fine. It's commercially dead. It doesn't convert, and it doesn't rank.

We built these skills because we got tired of fixing that output by hand. They encode the workflow we actually use on every Goliath client build, the taste, the structure, the SEO, the links, so an agent can produce work that looks like a real agency did it, not a chatbot.

Skill 01 — Agentic Web Design: the taste problem, solved.

The problem. Agents default to generic, committee-pleasing output. Ask one to "build a landing page" and you get a template. It's not ugly, it's worse. It's forgettable. It has no point of view, no hierarchy, no reason for a visitor to stay.

The solution, a workflow, not a prompt.

This skill turns "make it look good" into a repeatable process:

  • Feed the agent taste before it writes code. Point it at real design systems (awesome-design-md, Refero, Minimal Gallery) so it knows what good looks like before it starts.
  • Push past bland defaults. Eight techniques, seed strings, ambitious prompts, a design-critic subagent loop, image and video enrichment, that force genuinely creative output.
  • Build with real components. shadcn/ui, Aceternity, Magic UI, and the 3D stack (ThreeUI, Scrolltide, Canvas UI) instead of hand-rolled gradients.
  • Verify it converts. The 9-block landing page structure, the 25-point conversion audit, and the home-service playbook, the checks that separate a page that sells from one that just sits there.
Example

Ask the skill to build a landing page for a plumbing company in Calgary. It writes the PRD, pulls a real design system for taste, builds with shadcn components, then runs the 25-point audit before it calls it done. The result converts, because conversion was the spec, not an afterthought.

Skill 02 — SEO Ranking: the exact way we get to the top of Google.

The problem. A beautiful site that nobody finds is a brochure. Most agencies treat SEO as a black box, "we'll get you ranking," and deliver nothing you can verify. The process is actually knowable, and it's the same every time.

The solution, SEO as operating instructions.

This skill converts the Exposure Ninja playbook into a step-by-step process an agent can run:

  • Pick the right targets. Keyword research that starts from the business goal, not vanity volume. Map keywords to the pages that deserve to rank for them.
  • Analyze the competition. See exactly what Google is rewarding for each term, page type, content depth, links, reviews, and build the gap list.
  • Fix the foundations. On-page optimization, technical SEO, speed, mobile, schema, Search Console. The fast wins first.
  • Local SEO. Google Business Profile, citations, reviews, the local signals that put a service business on the map.
  • Measure in money. Organic leads, revenue, conversion rate, not vanity keyword counts.
Example

For a home-services client, the skill runs the full intake, builds the keyword-to-page map, drafts the service pages against the page-brief template, fixes the technical blockers, and sets up the monthly review loop. You see exactly what was done and what it produced, in numbers you can defend.

Skill 03 — Local Link Building: the part of Google most agencies skip.

The problem. You can have the best site and the best on-page SEO in your city, and still lose to a competitor with more authority. Links are how Google decides who to trust. And most agencies either ignore them or buy junk links that get you penalized.

The solution, earned links, on a 90-day plan.

This skill is the field guide we use for every local client. It's specialized for local service businesses, but the method applies to any business:

  • Fix your foundation first. Canonical NAP, claimed platforms, internal linking, and link reclamation, the highest-ROI work, zero outreach.
  • Inventory who already knows you. Suppliers, partners, testimonials, unlinked mentions. Links you already earned and never collected.
  • Join the local bodies. Chamber of commerce, associations, resource pages, sponsorships, local press with photos.
  • Run the 90-day plan. Two weeks of research, two weeks building the asset, then 15 to 30 genuine first contacts a week. Target: 5 to 15 real referring domains. That's a good result.
  • Never buy links. The one test: if you had to describe exactly what you did to Google, would you have anything to explain away?
Example

A plumbing client with a solid site but no authority. The skill runs the 90-day plan: reclaims lost links, gets the chamber listing, lands a local press story with photos, and earns 8 genuine referring domains in 16 weeks. The site outranks competitors who'd been at it for years.
